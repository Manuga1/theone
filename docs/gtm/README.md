# Padma go-to-market kit

Everything the two of you need to take Padma to market: the plan, the emails, the forms, the scripts and the rules. Written to be copied, filled in and sent.

Padma is a student project in pilot stage. It has no enrolled participants yet. Padma is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vanderbilt University or Vanderbilt University Medical Center.

1. Read `plan.md` (15 minutes). It is the approved plan. Every other file follows it. If a file here seems to disagree with the plan, the plan wins: tell me.
2. Read `guardrails.md` (10 minutes).
3. Hold the alignment call (`alignment-call.md`). Nothing is sent before it.

## Rules that apply to every file

- Fill in every [square bracket] before sending. If you cannot fill one honestly, delete the sentence.
- Say "research participants", never "patients".
- Interview requests quote no price and say the sender is a student doing discovery interviews.
- Anything with a price goes from the Padma domain, with the opt-out line and a postal address.
- Nothing goes from a VUMC address.
- No price goes to a university lab until the ethics office answers.
- No number from the simulated product goes into any email, document, slide or screenshot.
- You sign nothing and accept no money until a company and a business bank account exist.
- Files marked "Draft for legal review" are starting points for a lawyer, not finished documents.

## Setting up the tracker

1. Open `tracker-template.csv` in a spreadsheet in the Padma account. Restrict sharing to the two of you.
2. The allowed values for `track`, `status` and the yes/no columns are in `interview-guide.md`, under "Logging in the tracker".
3. One of you owns it (decided on the alignment call). Both update it the same day as any send, reply or call.
4. The tracker holds work contacts. It never holds registry members or anything about anyone's health.

## If the name changes

"Padma" is an existing brand in health products, and the name check may force a second word. If it does, the site changes in one place (`shared/brand.ts`). In this folder, search for "Padma" and replace it in every file except `plan.md`.

## How the target list was built, and how to extend it

Searches run on 12 November 2026. Re-run them, page further, and add rows to `targets.csv`. Take contact details from the public record yourself; never store them in this repository.

**CT.gov** (status Recruiting or NYR in every query; `cond` = condition, `intr` = intervention):
1. cond `prediabetes OR "type 2 diabetes" OR obesity`, intr `"continuous glucose monitor" OR CGM`, location Tennessee
2. cond `diabetes OR obesity OR hypertension OR sleep OR prediabetes`, intr `wearable OR "continuous glucose" OR "digital" OR "home blood pressure" OR actigraphy`, sponsor `Vanderbilt OR Meharry`
3. cond `prediabetes OR "healthy" OR "type 2 diabetes"`, intr `"continuous glucose monitoring" AND (diet OR meal OR nutrition)`, United States
4. Same theme, observational only: cond `prediabetes OR "healthy volunteers" OR "glucose metabolism" OR obesity OR "metabolic health"`, intr `"continuous glucose" OR wearable`
5. cond hypertension/sleep/diabetes/obesity, intr `wearable OR smartwatch OR "home blood pressure" OR "remote monitoring" OR actigraphy OR "Apple Watch" OR Fitbit`, location `Tennessee OR Kentucky OR Georgia OR Alabama OR "North Carolina" OR Ohio`
6. Investigator search: institution Vanderbilt, cond `diabetes OR obesity OR hypertension OR sleep OR prediabetes`, page size 60
7. Sleep, observational: intr `wearable OR actigraphy OR "Oura" OR smartwatch OR "sleep tracker"`
8. cond `healthy OR prediabetes OR cardiometabolic`, intr `"digital biomarker" OR wearable OR "remote monitoring" OR decentralized OR smartphone`, plus `AREA[StartDate]RANGE[2025-01-01,MAX]`
9. Query 5 conditions in the six states, plus `AREA[StartDate]RANGE[2024-06-01,MAX]`
10. `AREA[LeadSponsorClass]INDUSTRY AND AREA[StartDate]RANGE[2025-01-01,MAX]`, intr `sensor OR wearable OR "continuous glucose" OR cuffless OR "noninvasive glucose" OR ring OR patch`
11. Industry sponsors with phase NA, cond `glucose OR glycemic OR postprandial OR "personalized nutrition" OR "metabolic health" OR prediabetes`
12. CGM with `AREA[HealthyVolunteers]true`

I opened the full record for 11 trials. Everything marked n/r needs `get_trial_details` (or a look on clinicaltrials.gov) to get the PI and city.

**PubMed** (all limited to 2025–2026):
1. `(continuous glucose monitoring) AND (wearable OR smartwatch) AND (diet OR meal OR food) AND (machine learning OR prediction OR personalized)` — 32 hits
2. `(free-living OR real-world) AND multimodal AND wearable AND (dataset OR cohort) AND (glucose OR metabolic OR cardiometabolic)` — 7 hits
3. `(postprandial glucose response prediction) AND (personalized nutrition) AND (continuous glucose monitor)` — 20 hits
4. `(continuous glucose monitoring OR wearable) AND (prediabetes OR "without diabetes" OR "healthy adults") AND (machine learning OR digital biomarker) AND (USA[Affiliation] OR "United States"[Affiliation])` — 16 hits
5. `(Vanderbilt[Affiliation] OR Meharry[Affiliation]) AND (wearable OR "continuous glucose" OR "digital biomarker" OR "home blood pressure" OR actigraphy OR Fitbit)` — **117 hits, only the first 25 reviewed**
