**Draft for legal review. Not legal advice.**

This is a plain statement of what the two of you intend. It is not a contract and it does not form a company. Its job is to stop surprises. A lawyer turns it into formation documents when the company is formed (after Gate B in `plan.md`). Fill in the [brackets], sign and date it, and each keep a copy.

Padma is a student project. It is not a company. It has no enrolled participants, no customers and no revenue. Neither of us may sign for Padma or accept money for Padma until a company and a business bank account exist.

### 3. Ownership (provisional)

- Intended split when the company is formed: [Founder 1] [__]% and [Founder 2] [__]%.
- Both shares vest over time. Proposed: [__ years, with nothing vested before __ months]. A lawyer sets the final terms.
- Until formation this split is an intention only. Either of us may ask to revisit it once before formation, in writing.
- Shares or options for later team members come out of a pool agreed at formation, not from one founder's share.

### 4. What belongs to the future company

We both agree that these belong to the company once it exists, and that neither of us owns them personally in the meantime:

- all code in the Padma repository, wherever it is hosted;
- the registry list and the sheet that holds it;
- the outreach tracker, interview notes and target lists;
- the domain, the mailbox and any accounts opened for Padma;
- the name "Padma" and any logo or wording made for it;
- every document in `docs/gtm/`.

At formation we each sign an assignment that moves our work on Padma into the company. Until then, the repository, domain and accounts stay in [whose account], and the other founder has [admin / owner] access.

### 5. What does not belong to Padma

- Work done for our labs, classes or clinical training.
- Anything made with lab time, lab equipment, institutional data or clinical access. We do not use any of these for Padma.
- Work on [other venture name], which stays separate (see section 7).

### 6. If one of us leaves

- That person tells the other in writing.
- Their access to the registry sheet, tracker, domain, mailbox and repository ends that day.
- The registry list stays with Padma. The person leaving keeps no copy.
- Vested ownership is kept; unvested ownership returns to the company. Before formation, we agree a fair share based on [months worked / hours logged].
- The person leaving does not contact registry members or prospects on Padma's behalf afterwards.
