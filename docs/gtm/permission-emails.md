# The three permission emails (send Friday 13 November)

Three short emails, each with a numbered list of questions. Send all three on Friday. Campus recruiting and any priced offer to a university lab wait for the written answers.

## Rules for all three

- Hold the alignment call first (`alignment-call.md`).
- Nothing goes from a VUMC address.
- Quote no price. Attach nothing. Link to nothing in the first email.
- Fill in every [bracket]. Check each office's current contact page for the right address; do not guess one.
- Log each email in the tracker with `track` = `permission`.
- Save every reply as a PDF in a shared folder. A written yes is the only yes that counts.

## Email 1: research-ethics office (HRPP)

**Subject:** Student question: does a contact-only volunteer registry need a determination?

```text
Dear HRPP team,

I am an undergraduate at Vanderbilt. A classmate and I run a student project called Padma. It is not yet a company and is not affiliated with the university.

We keep a registry of adults who agree to be emailed about research studies they might join. It holds contact details only (name, email, age band, state, devices owned, kinds of study of interest) and no health information. We do not run a study ourselves.

Before we approach any Vanderbilt or VUMC lab, may we ask three questions?

1. Does a contact-only registry run by students need a determination from your office?
2. May a lab pay an outside, student-owned recruiter to send its approved study notice to registry members? If so, how should the payment be set up and disclosed?
3. What must the lab's amendment contain to name Padma as a recruitment source?

We will offer no paid service to a university lab until we hear from you. If another office should answer, please say which.

Thank you,
[Your name]
[Year and major]
```

## Email 2: Dean of Students, and Wond'ry programme leads

**Subject:** Student venture: three questions before we use any campus channel

```text
Dear Dean of Students office,

I am an undergraduate. A classmate and I run a student project called Padma. It is not yet a company.

Padma keeps a registry of adults who agree to be emailed about research studies they might join. It collects contact details only and no health information. So far we have told only people we know personally. We will use no campus channel until we have your answer in writing.

Our questions:

1. May a student venture invite people to this registry through campus channels (a reserved table, posters, a student-organisation newsletter)? If so, which approval route should we follow?
2. May our page say that Padma is "run by Vanderbilt students", with no logo and a clear line that the university does not endorse it?
3. May we send discovery-interview requests to researchers from our vanderbilt.edu addresses? They quote no price and say we are students.

We understand that door-to-door recruiting and flyering in residence halls are not allowed, and we will do neither.

Thank you,
[Your name]
[Year and major]
```
