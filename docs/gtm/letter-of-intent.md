# Letter of intent (non-binding)

**Draft for legal review. Not legal advice.**

How to use this template:

- Use it when a buyer wants to show intent before Padma's company exists, or before their ethics cover is in place.
- It binds nobody. It carries no price unless the buyer has already received a proposal from the Padma domain.
- **Before the company exists, only the buyer signs.** The founders sign nothing. They acknowledge the letter by email. Once the company exists, it may countersign.
- No deposit is taken with this letter. Padma accepts no money before the company and its bank account exist.
- Do not send it to a university lab before the ethics office has answered.
- A signed letter counts as a demand signal for Gate B only if it names a study, a budget and a purchasing route.

---

[Buyer letterhead or name]

[Date]

To: Padma, a student project run by [founder names] ("Padma")

**Letter of intent: [short name of the study or pilot]**

1. **Who we are.** [Buyer legal name] ("we") [one sentence on what the buyer does].

2. **What we intend.** We intend to work with Padma on a pilot in which Padma helps us reach adults who have agreed to be invited to research studies. The pilot we have in mind is: [Offer A, referral campaign / Offer B, pilot cohort of about __ completers / Offer C, design partner with 10 participants], using [protocol sheet name or one-line description], starting around [month and year].

3. **The study and its budget.** The pilot would support [study name or product]. The budget we expect to use is [budget line or amount], and the person who approves this purchase is [name and role].

4. **Conditions.** This intention depends on all of the following:
   - ethics approval, or written confirmation from our counsel that the work is not research, before any participant is contacted;
   - a definitive written agreement signed by us and by the company that Padma's founders form;
   - [our supplier onboarding process / any other condition].

5. **Recontact.** We [are / are not] interested in a separately priced option to ask the same participants for one more measurement later.

6. **What we understand about Padma.** Padma is a student project in pilot stage. It has no enrolled participants yet and is not yet a company. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vanderbilt University or Vanderbilt University Medical Center. Any data collected under our protocol belongs to our study. Padma does not hold participant money and does not buy devices.

7. **Not binding.** This letter states our present intention only. It creates no obligation on us or on Padma to sign an agreement, to pay or to deliver anything. Either side may withdraw at any time by email. Each side bears its own costs.

8. **Confidentiality.** Padma may tell advisers, grant programmes and prospective team members that this letter exists and who signed it. Padma will not publish it or use our name or logo in public material without our written permission.

9. **Timing.** We expect to decide by [date]. This letter lapses on [date, suggested 90 days] unless both sides agree in writing to extend it.

Signed for [Buyer legal name]:

Name: ____________________ Title: ____________________

Signature: ____________________ Date: ____________________
