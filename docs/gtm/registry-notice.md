# Registry privacy notice and joining terms

**Draft for legal review. Not legal advice.**

Two texts:

1. **The notice.** It is shown on the site at `/privacy`. The site page is the published version; this file is the working copy. If you change one, change the other, and raise the version number and date in both.
2. **The short terms.** A person ticks these when they join. Paste them into the registry form (see `form-fields.md`).

Read both aloud once before the registry opens. If a sentence is not true on the day, change the sentence or change what you do.

Until a company exists, the two named founders hold this list personally and are personally answerable for what this notice promises.

---

## 1. Registry privacy notice

**Version 0.2, 12 November 2026.**

This notice covers the Padma registry: the list of people who asked to be invited to research studies. It is short on purpose. Please read it before you sign up.

### Who runs Padma

Padma is a student project run by [Founder 1 full name] and [Founder 2 full name]. It is not yet a company. Padma is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vanderbilt University or Vanderbilt University Medical Center.

Padma is a student project in pilot stage. It has no enrolled participants yet.

### What we collect, and why

When you join we ask for:

- your name and email address, so we can write to you;
- your age band, the state you live in and the state you would be in during a study, because studies have rules about who can take part and where;
- the devices you own and the kinds of study you would consider, so we only send invitations that could fit you.

We do not ask for health information, and we ask you not to send any.

We will email you when a study might fit you. Now and then we may also send a short check-in asking you to confirm your details are still right. It never asks about your health, and you can ignore it.

### Studies, consent and pay

Joining the registry does not put you in a study. Each study has its own consent form and states its pay before you decide. Nothing is guaranteed: you may not be invited, a study may be full, and no payment is promised by joining.

### Who sees your details

Your details are never sold. A study team receives them only if you say yes to that study. Until then they stay with us.

### No advertising trackers

We do not use advertising trackers on this site or in the registry. The sign-up form is run by a form service, which operates under its own terms.

### How your details are kept

They are kept in a restricted spreadsheet protected by two-factor sign-in. Two named people can open it: [Founder 1 full name] and [Founder 2 full name]. Nobody else has access.

### Leaving and deletion

You can leave at any time. Email [contact email] and we will delete your details within 7 days.

### If Padma becomes a company

If the project becomes a company, we will tell you before your details move to it, so you can leave first if you prefer.

### Who can join

US residents aged 18 or older (19 or older in Alabama and Nebraska, 21 or older in Mississippi).

### Contact

[Contact email]

## What you must do to keep it true

| Promise | What it takes |
|---|---|
| "Two named people can open it" | Exactly two accounts on the sheet. Check the sharing settings every Monday |
| "Two-factor sign-in" | Both accounts have it switched on. No shared password |
| "Never sold"; "only if you say yes" | No export of the list to anyone. A study team gets one person's details only after that person replies yes to that study's notice |
| "Delete within 7 days" | One named owner checks the mailbox daily, deletes the row, and replies to confirm. Keep a count of deletions, not the names |
| "No advertising trackers" | No analytics or pixel scripts added to the site or the form. No ad-platform audiences built from the list |
| "Told before details move to a company" | Email every member before the move, with a simple way to leave and enough time to use it (suggested: 14 days; ask the lawyer) |
| "No health information" | If someone sends health information by email, do not copy it into the sheet. Reply asking them not to, and delete the email |
| Age rules | Remove any row that breaks the state age rule (see `form-fields.md`) and tell the person why |
