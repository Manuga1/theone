# Pilot proposal: one page (Offer B or Offer C)

**Draft for legal review. Not legal advice.**

How to use this template:

- Send it only when a buyer has asked for a written proposal.
- Startups only for now. Do not send a priced proposal to a university lab until the ethics office has answered.
- Fill in the [brackets], delete these instructions and the notes in italics, save as a PDF, and send it from the Padma domain with the cover note in `outreach-templates.md` (section E).
- It is a proposal. Nothing is signed before a company exists.
- Put no number from the simulated product in it.

---

## Padma pilot proposal

**Proposal date:** [date] **Valid until:** [date, 30 days later] **Version:** [1]

**This is a proposal, not a contract. Padma is a student project in pilot stage. It has no enrolled participants yet. Padma is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vanderbilt University or Vanderbilt University Medical Center.**

### 1. Parties

- **Buyer:** [legal name of the company or lab], [address]. Contact: [name, role, email].
- **Padma:** a student project run by [founder names]. It is not yet a company. If the buyer accepts this proposal, the agreement will be made with the company the founders form, [company name, when known].

### 2. Purpose

[One or two sentences in the buyer's words: what they want to learn and why. Example: "To collect 14 days of everyday-life data from adults who use the buyer's app, to check how well the app's sleep estimate matches what people report."]

The purpose is research or product feedback only. It is none of the uses on Padma's banned list (section 10).

### 3. Offer

- **Offer B, pilot cohort:** [15–25] completers on the protocol in section 5.
- **Offer C, design partner:** 10 participants on the protocol in section 5, with a weekly 30-minute call.

### 4. Who takes part

- Adults who meet the criteria in the buyer's protocol: [criteria as written in the protocol].
- US residents aged 18 or older (19 or older in Alabama and Nebraska, 21 or older in Mississippi). [States included: ____.]
- People are invited from the Padma registry, where they opted in to this kind of study, and through study-specific recruiting agreed with the buyer: [channels].
- Registry members are leads, not a cohort. Padma does not promise a number of people from the registry.

### 5. Protocol

- Protocol sheet: [Lifestyle 14 / Sleep and stress 14], version [date], attached. *The sheets are on the site under /protocols. The home blood pressure add-on is for a second pilot only.*
- Measures, schedule and deliverable are as on the sheet, changed only as listed here: [changes, or "none"].
- Devices: participants use their own phone and their own wearable. No devices are supplied in a first pilot.

### 6. Ethics cover

[Choose one and delete the others.]

- The buyer holds an approved protocol: [review board, protocol number, approval date]. It names Padma as a recruitment source and describes what Padma does.
- The buyer's counsel treats this work as product feedback and not as research. The buyer confirms this in writing: [name of counsel, date].
- Neither applies yet. Independent ethics review is added: about four more weeks, and the review fee is paid by the buyer directly to the review board.

No participant is contacted until this section is complete.

### 7. What Padma does

- Sends the buyer's approved study notice to registry members who opted in to this kind of study.
- Runs the study-specific recruiting in section 4.
- Sends reminders and answers practical questions from participants, only as the buyer's protocol allows.
- Reports weekly: how many notices were sent, how many people clicked through, how many started and how many finished. Counts only.
- At the end, delivers what the protocol sheet lists under "What you receive". [Choose one. (a) The buyer's team exports the study data from its own tool; Padma delivers the completion report. (b) The buyer's protocol names Padma's founders as study staff; they prepare the files, the data dictionary and the completion report inside the buyer's tool.]
- [Offer C: joins a weekly call.]
- [If the recontact option is taken: section 11.]

### 8. What Padma does not do

- Does not decide who is eligible, and does not collect health information to screen anyone.
- Does not take consent. Consent is taken under the buyer's protocol, by the buyer's staff or tool.
- Does not keep study data. Study data is collected in the buyer's tool and stays there. Under option (b) in section 7, the founders work inside that tool as named study staff, and no copy leaves it. *Option (b) means the founders take part in the research. Take advice before agreeing to it, and never put study data into Padma's own software.*
- Does not hold participant money, and does not buy or ship devices.
- Does not give medical advice or interpret any reading.
- Does not promise that a set number of people will enrol or finish.

### 9. What the buyer provides

- The approved protocol and consent form, or the written confirmation in section 6.
- The study notice Padma may send, in its approved wording.
- The tool that collects consent and study data: [tool].
- Participant compensation, paid by the buyer directly to participants: [amount per participant, schedule and method, as stated in the consent form].
- A named contact for participant questions about the study: [name].
- [Second pilot only: devices, bought and shipped by the buyer, and a safety and incidental-findings plan.]

### 10. Banned uses

The buyer will not use anything from this pilot for advertising or marketing targeting, insurance underwriting, employment decisions, credit or lending, housing, political targeting, law enforcement or immigration, re-identification, resale to data brokers, litigation against participants, or gambling or other targeting of behavioural vulnerability.

[In / Out.] If in: after the pilot ends, Padma asks the same participants for one more measurement: [what, when]. This must be described in the buyer's protocol and consent form before the pilot starts. Only participants who agreed to be contacted again are asked.

- Payment terms: [startup: __% of the service fee on signature, the balance on the final report. University: invoiced against a purchase order on delivery.] *The percentage is not set in the plan; agree it with a lawyer.*

- Every fee here is a starting point. Padma expects to adjust it with the buyer before signing.

### 14. What happens to data

- Study data belongs to the buyer's study and is used only as the consent form allows. Padma keeps no copy and gains no right to reuse, license or show it.
- Padma keeps its registry (contact details only) under its own privacy notice. The buyer receives a person's contact details only when that person says yes to this study.
- Padma keeps the counts in its weekly reports and may say, with the buyer's written permission, that the pilot took place.
- If a participant withdraws, the buyer's protocol decides what happens to their study data. Padma stops contacting them about the study at once.
